About this calculator

How the numbers are worked out, and what to keep in mind.

Formulas

SGPA (one semester)

SGPA = Σ (credits × grade points) ÷ Σ credits

Only subjects that carry credits are counted. Audit / non-credit courses (0 credits, e.g. Sports & Yoga, Constitution of India) are shown but left out.

CGPA (all semesters)

CGPA = Σ over all semesters (credits × grade points) ÷ Σ all credits

CGPA is credit-weighted, so it is not just the average of your SGPAs.

Percentage

Each university has its own conversion. For AKTU:

Percentage = (CGPA × 10) − 7.5

The formula for your university is shown in the calculator and on the Grade Table page.

Worked example

Subjects with 4, 3 and 1 credits graded A (9), B+ (8) and A+ (10):

SGPA = (4×9 + 3×8 + 1×10) ÷ (4+3+1) = 70 ÷ 8 = 8.75

FAQ

Does an F grade count?

Yes. F is worth 0 grade points, but its credits still count in the total — so it pulls your SGPA down until you clear the subject.

Why are two subjects shown in one row, e.g. “Engineering Physics / Engineering Chemistry”?

AKTU splits students into groups that take one of two subjects in a semester and the other one next semester. Both options carry the same credits, so pick the grade for the one you took.

What does the yellow “unverified” warning mean?

That semester's subjects or the grade scale haven't been checked against the official university document yet. The result may not match your marksheet.

Where is my data stored?

Only in your own browser (localStorage). Nothing about your grades is sent to the server. Use “Reset” to clear it.

My university or branch isn't listed.

Only universities whose syllabus has been added are available. New ones can be added as JSON files on the backend — see the project README.

Disclaimer

This calculator gives estimates for planning only. Syllabus data is taken from university PDFs and may contain mistakes or be out of date. Always confirm your SGPA, CGPA and percentage with your official university result and the latest ordinance.
